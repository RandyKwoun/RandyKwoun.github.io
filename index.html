About me.

Hi, I’m Randy Kwoun, a game programmer specializing in enemy AI and UI. I design enemies that read the situation, move with purpose, and attack accordingly, and I build UI that feels natural whether you’re playing with a controller or a keyboard and mouse. I mainly work in Unity and C#. I first got into game development in 2023 and transferred to USC as a Computer Science (Games) major in 2025. In the summer of 2026, I interned as a UI Engineer at MGK Studio, working on Sledge Hammer Bride. My goal is to make games that feel fun from the very first input.
