About.

You’re a tomb raider who’s just pulled a priceless treasure from deep inside a pyramid. There’s only one problem: it’s cursed. Now you’ve got a choice to make, the gold or your life, and staying alive means putting the treasure back where you found it. Before the pyramid decides you’re never leaving.

My Contributions.

Enemy AI

Built mummy and skeleton AI that patrol the pyramid and chase the player once spotted, using a state machine to switch smoothly between behaviours.

Hiding System

Implemented a system that lets players hide inside sarcophagi to escape pursuing enemies. When the player hides, they’re repositioned into the sarcophagus and become undetectable to enemies, turning a tense chase into a chance to breathe.

Torch

Implemented a torch the player can raise and lower at will, lighting up the pyramid’s dark corridors as they explore.

Torch Puzzle

Designed a brazier puzzle with no hints and real consequences. Light the right braziers and a hidden door opens. Light the wrong one and you might unleash mummies and skeletons, or trigger spikes from the walls.
